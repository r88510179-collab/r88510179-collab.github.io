3. Ruling

Only the actions valid for the incident's current state are offered, and only the consequences its policy permits. An absent-game action records the commissioner's attestation that the game is absent from its original week's feed; no other week's game is ever linked to it.

4. Preview and confirm

What the public pages will show before and after this ruling, computed with the same evaluator they run.

Before the write, the score feed, the published week, the policy and the ruling chain are read again; if anything material changed nothing is sent.

Rulings are append-only: a withdrawal or re-ruling adds a row and never edits or deletes an earlier one. Private notes are stored for the commissioner and never reach participants.
